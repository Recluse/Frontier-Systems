## Membership introduction

Support steady development of Frontier Systems. The current game is a cooperative engineering prototype, with finite materials, modular machinery, physical construction and shared-world checkpoint recovery. Monthly support helps fund implementation, testing and readable progress reports.

Members receive development context and optional recognition. Public milestones and honest limitations remain visible to everyone. Membership does not purchase a finished game, exclusive gameplay advantages or control over the roadmap.

## Frontier Supporter — $5/month

Help keep development moving. Receive a monthly supporter roundup with verified progress, selected prototype screenshots and the next bounded goals. If progress is delayed, the roundup explains the blocker instead of promising a feature that is not ready.

## Workshop Supporter — $15/month

Includes the monthly roundup, plus a monthly behind-the-scenes engineering note explaining one design choice, test or recovery problem. Members may suggest topics; suggestions do not guarantee implementation or a personal response deadline.

## Frontier Patron — $30/month

Includes the roundup and engineering note, plus optional recognition on the project's supporter acknowledgement page under a display name you approve. Anonymous support is welcome. No personal contact information is published. Recognition is handled in a monthly batch; it does not grant in-game benefits.

## Delivery and boundaries

All benefits are digital. First monthly roundup is due within 30 days of campaign launch, then monthly; higher-tier engineering notes follow the same cadence. Do not offer tier benefits until this delivery capacity is admitted. Existing source remains governed by its published MIT license. No game keys, physical rewards, scheduled calls, priority bug fixes, lifetime access or guaranteed early access are included. Membership prices are not changed without Coordinator review and necessary owner approval.
