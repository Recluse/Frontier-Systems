### Confirmed wording

**Frontier Systems**

I'm working on a cooperative engineering game in Godot, with Civ13 and SS13 as references for the kind of depth I want to build toward. If you enjoy Nomads, making things from raw materials, or figuring out how machinery works, this may be worth following.

The source prototype already has a basic loop: extract finite material, carry it in containers, refine it, craft plates, build and dismantle walls, and recycle what you recover. It also has modular machinery, nested inventory, save/reconnect recovery, and tests with two authenticated local clients.

It's early, and the visuals are still basic. There isn't a packaged demo or public playtest yet. Colonies, spacecraft and broader civilization/station systems are planned work, not features I'm claiming are finished. The immediate focus is making the basic engineering loop usable.

I'd like the game to eventually help sustain my open source work, including DiscordPHP. The current support campaign is for initial game development. If you'd like to follow along, contribute, or help fund that work:

Project: http://frontier-systems.discordphp.org/
Source and prototype instructions: https://github.com/Valgorithms/Frontier-Systems
Progress updates and support: https://ko-fi.com/valgorithms

Ideas and feedback are welcome, especially from people who like the building and engineering side of Civ13.
