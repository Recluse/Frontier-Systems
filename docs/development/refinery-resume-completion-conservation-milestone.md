# Refinery Resume completion conservation milestone

Tests/docs successor to merged PR102 (`041080b5af6853712ca3376a74818f15e32fb264`). The fixture reuses the finite paid refinery setup and native checkpoint/manual reconnect transport helpers. The existing Cancel fixture exposes its command-only preparation as a reusable helper; its acceptance behavior remains intact. No runtime, schema or UI changes.

## Acceptance and canonical contract

Production owns machine progress and energy at the fixed simulation tick, using production snapshot v7 and observation v8. Native whole-world persistence validates production and inventory provenance before restore, and pauses active machine jobs. Inputs stay in physical canisters until the atomic completion tick.

The inherited paid finite loop prepares exactly 1,000,000 mg ice in `resource-canister-1` and an empty `resource-canister-2`, with both inserted in the existing refinery through authoritative commands. The actual client starts `refine.ice` (60 ticks, 500 prototype J), saves its partly paid job through worker-completed native storage, loses a pending Resume before host ingress, and manually reconnects the same running client after in-process host replacement. Reconnect sends no abandoned command or implicit batch replay. The restored job remains paused for 180 ticks with exact saved recipe, work, spent energy, cargo and physical inventory records.

Explicit Resume freezes only machine/revision/request identity and preserves `refine.ice` despite an unrelated selected dropdown recipe. One accepted command creates one authenticated-slot-bound receipt and advances production revision once. Resume adds only the next work/energy increment to the saved progress. A genuinely restored paused clone supplies grid-only charging baselines; Resume and the final tick debit only the unpaid energy increments and refund none.

Through the penultimate work tick, complete canister records match the original unconsumed input. The final tick atomically consumes 1,000,000 mg ice and produces 1,000,000 mg water, removes the job, advances production revision once more and emits one completion event. Sorted carrier allocation reuses the now-empty `resource-canister-1` for water and leaves `resource-canister-2` empty. Assertions independently require both complete records and masses, unchanged deposits/storage/other matter, machinery provenance, workstation reservations, and the entire inventory owner graph and revisions. JSON round trips compare persisted records consistently across integer/JSON-number representations.

Later idle ticks, identical retained Resume intent, changed intent under its retained ID and a fresh absent-job Resume cannot create more output, receipts, revisions, inventory mutations or completion events. Every probe compares all machinery placements to a genuine grid-only baseline, distinguishing ordinary generation from an energy refund. The actual client then saves the completed world through native worker storage. A second in-process host replacement/manual reconnect retains the complete production record and receipt. Idle ticks and both retained/fresh Resume probes after that restore cannot recreate the completed job or duplicate conversion.
